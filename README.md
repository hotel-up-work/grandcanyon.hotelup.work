# GRAND CANYON

Live site: https://grandcanyon.hotelup.work

## About
GRAND CANYON is a small guesthouse (12 rooms) located at vul. Dovha 1/7 in Kamianets-Podilskyi, Ukraine. It positions itself as a compact alternative to a large hotel complex, combining a cozy home-like stay with an active travel format: guests can relax on the terrace, enjoy BBQ evenings, and explore the city and its canyon-side landscapes on foot or by bike. The property also mentions an unconventional offering called "vulyko-terapiya" (beehive therapy), though the page notes that details, seasonality, and rules should be confirmed directly with the owners, and it makes no medical or health claims about it.

## Amenities
- Wi-Fi on the property
- On-site parking
- Terrace
- BBQ area
- Gazebos (альтанки)
- Shared lounge / rest room
- Excursions (organized by prior arrangement)
- Bike rides
- 12 guest rooms, including a standard double room, a two-room "fireplace hall" with its own terrace, and attic-style/upgraded rooms with a view of the canyon
